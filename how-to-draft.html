Tournament guide

How to draft

Captains build their squads live on the tournament page. Here is how it works, then a practice draft you can play through against two bots. Nothing here touches the real tournament.

01

Log in with Twitch

Captains log in on the tournament page. Twitch tells the site who you are, so there is no password.

02

Wait for the draft to open

The board is locked until the admin opens it. Keep the page open: you get a beep and a flashing tab when it is your turn.

03

Pick when you are on the clock

Your banner turns red with a countdown. Tap Draft next to a player in the pool, then Yes to confirm.

04

Snake order

Round 1 goes in the listed order. Round 2 flips it, round 3 flips back. First pick in round 1 is last pick in round 2.

05

Do not run out the clock

If your time hits zero the admin can auto-pick the best available player for you. It is better to pick yourself.

06

Name your team

Tap the pencil next to your team name on the board to rename your squad. Only you and the admin can.
