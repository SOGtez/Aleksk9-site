## The format

The **R6 5v5 Tournament**, a one-time event co-hosted by AleksK9 and Notvash30, had six captains draft their teams live on this site in a snake draft: each team picked one player from every tier, so every roster was built the same way. Matches were first to 7 rounds, with overtime first to 8 at 6-6.

The playoffs were single elimination. Seeds 1 and 2 got a bye to the semifinals, every match was a Bo1, and the final was a Bo3.

## How it went

- **Quarterfinal 1:** Team Alfie beat val gf 8–6 on Chalet
- **Quarterfinal 2:** Team Frankie beat Team Niko 7–1 on Clubhouse
- **Semifinal 1:** Team Alfie beat Team Pocket 7–2 on Nighthaven Labs
- **Semifinal 2:** Team Frankie beat Team MrOctober 7–4 on Clubhouse
- **Final:** Team Alfie beat Team Frankie

Congratulations to **Team Alfie**, and thanks to every captain, player and helper who made it happen. The full bracket is still up at [aleksk9.com/bracket](/bracket).
