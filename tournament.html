      How it works

      01

      Two captains, snake draft

      Captains log in with Twitch and pick from the player pool on this page. Round 1 goes A then B. Round 2 flips to B then A, and it keeps alternating until both rosters are full.

      02

      Best of three

      5v5 Rainbow Six Siege, first team to win two maps takes the series. Scores update here as the maps are played.

      03

      Stats after every map

      Kills, deaths, and assists are entered from the in-game scoreboard after each map and roll up into the leaderboard above.
